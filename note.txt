In OAuth2 there is multiple grant_type flows
    1- client_credentials (used when auth needed between 2 systems & backend integrations)
        EX: Rajhi Bank System needs to Generate token from IVP first to talk to Yakeen and it gets validated by Yakeen from IVP
    2- authorization_code (used by the client like registering to new website using google account)
